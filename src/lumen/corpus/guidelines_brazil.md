# Brazil market guidelines

These rules apply when the policyholder is located in Brazil.

## Cancellation notice period

The policyholder must give at least 60 days written notice to cancel. Lumen applies the cancellation
on the 61st day after receipt and sends written confirmation within 1 business day.

## Claim filing window

A claim must be filed within 365 days of the date of loss. A claim filed after day 365 is denied with
`claim_late`, and the denial notice must be delivered in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Right of withdrawal

The policyholder may withdraw within 7 calendar days after the policy documents are delivered, with
no penalty and no reason required. Lumen sends the withdrawal receipt within 5 business days.

## Data residency

Primary policy data for Brazilian policyholders is stored in the `sa-east` region and replicated only
inside Brazil. No Brazilian policy data may be transferred to another region or to another country.

## Complaint response SLA

A complaint is acknowledged within 1 business day and resolved within 10 business days. Once the
policyholder escalates, Lumen has 15 days to send a final written position to the regulator.

## Waiting period for pre-existing conditions

Covered conditions that existed before inception carry a waiting period of 24 months, which group policies may reduce to 180 days.

## Portuguese disclosure requirement

All policy documents, endorsements and claim correspondence must be in Brazilian Portuguese, and a translation may not replace it.
